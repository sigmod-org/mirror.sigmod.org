Conference Venue

The 2015 ACM SIGMOD/PODS Conference will be held at Melbourne Convention & Exhibition Centre (1 Convention Centre Place, South Wharf, VIC, Australia). MCEC is centrally located, and is accessible by taxi, tram, train and bus. Please visit http://mcec.com.au/transport-options/ for more information about the transportation options to the venue.

Accommodations

Special rates have been negotiated for conference delegates at hotels close to the conference venue. These rates are only available to conference delegates and bookings MUST be made via the registration process via the Conference Organisers. Do not contact the hotels directly to book these rates. Every effort is made to satisfy accommodation requests and we strongly encourage you to make your accommodation booking as early as possible to avoid disappointment and secure these special conference rates. All rooms at the hotels listed on this page will be available at the advertised rate until Thursday, 30 April 2015; after this date rooms will be subject to availability.

Prices listed are in Australian dollars and are per room per night. Prices are room only rates unless otherwise specified.

Please note: Credit card details are required to secure your booking. These details will officially confirm your booking. Each guest is responsible for settling their own account upon check-out.

Should you wish to extend your stay beyond the conference dates, please email the Conference Organisers at sigmodpods2015reg@iceaustralia.com advising of the check-in and check-out dates and the room type you require. We will then make the booking on your behalf.
