2006 ACM SIGMOD International Conference on Management of Data

25th ACM SIGMOD-SIGACT-SIGART Symposium on Principles of Database Systems

The annual ACM SIGMOD conference is a leading international forum for database researchers, developers, and users to explore cutting-edge ideas and results, and to exchange techniques, tools, and experiences. The PODS symposium series, held in conjunction with the SIGMOD conference series, provides a premier annual forum for the communication of new advances in the theoretical foundation of database systems.
