Conference Banquet - Boat Cruise
Wednesday, 18:30

This year's SIGMOD/PODS banquet will take a place on the Mystic Blue boat. The cruise will take approximately 3 hours and give the participants an opportunity to socialize, while looking at the breath-taking views of the world-renowned Chicago Skyline. The Mystic Blue will be boarding at the Navy Pier, a very charming and entertaining area by itself. Navy Pier is approximately 1.3 miles east of the conference venue, and convenient bus transportation will be provided for the participants, boarding at the Renaissance hotel.
