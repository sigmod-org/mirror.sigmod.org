Panel Proposal Formatting Instructions

Panels should address timely and, preferably, controversial issues and must be debate-oriented rather than series of short presentations. A proposal should include a topic title; a short statement about the importance and relevance of the panel and the potential issues of controversy; a tentative list of questions that will be posed to the panelists; a list of confirmed participants along with their affiliations; and a brief bio of each participant. Proposals must be submitted electronically in PDF format by November 29, 2005 9pm Pacific Time to Christian S. Jensen. Short panel summaries will appear in the proceedings.
