Industrial Exhibits

Call for Industrial Exhibits

The SIGMOD/PODS Industrial Exhibits Program is a new forum that provides companies with the opportunity to interactively demonstrate products resulting from their database systems research. For a relatively low cost, companies can reach prospective clients in a one-on-one setting and show off the salient features of their information system products. SIGMOD/PODS will select a small number of companies for the Industrial Exhibits program, on a first come, first served basis. Contact Dr. Rafael Alonso at ralonso@sarnoff.com.
