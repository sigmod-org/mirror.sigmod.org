ACM SIGMOD/PODS 2021 will be a virtual conference with a local event at Xi'an, Shaanxi, China


Welcome

Welcome to the home of the 2021 ACM SIGMOD/PODS Conference, which was supposed to be held in Xi'an, Shaanxi, China, on June 20 - June 25, 2021. However, due to global uncertainties, travel restrictions, and other potential difficulties associated with COVID’19, SIGMOD’21 is transitioning to an online format. The online conference will be complemented with a local physical event at Xi’an, primarily targeting our strong data management community in China.

See more information about the local event.

SIGMOD/PODS 2021 Online Conference Planning

We are introducing several new ideas to increase interactivity and make it easier to participate online. These include 1) Thematically Curated Sessions which are mini-workshops of papers across all tracks on similar topics, 2) Office Hours, 3) Joint Q&A panels, 4) Running all sessions twice 12 hours apart to enable ease of participation from around the world, and much more. You can find more information on the details of our online planning here.


SIGMOD/PODS 2021 Anti-Harassment Policy

SIGMOD 2021 adheres to the ACM Policy Against Harassment. Any participant who experiences unacceptable behavior may contact any current member of the SIGMOD Executive Committee and the PODS Executive Committee or DBCares. Please be assured that if you approach us, your concerns will be kept in strict confidence, and we will consult with you on any actions taken.
