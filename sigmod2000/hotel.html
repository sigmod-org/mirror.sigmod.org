Hotel Information:

  • NEW ! The Adam's Mark hotel has already given up all booked rooms at this time, however we have just reached agreements with the following hotels:
    • Magnolia Hotel (tel: 214-915-6500, fax: 214-752-0558), fixed rate of $142, and
    • Dallas Grand Hotel (800-421-0011, fax: 214-747-1342), current rate of $75 (we are trying to get it lower to $59 in order to make it more affordable for students).
  • The conference will be held at the Adam's Mark Dallas Hotel in downtown Dallas, 10 minutes  from Love Field Airport, 30 minutes from Dallas-Ft. Worth Airport. Phone: 214-922-8000. Fax: 214-922-0308.
  • Toll-Free Reservations: 800-444-ADAM (2326). Make sure you mention ACM SIGMOD/PODS Conference to get the special group rates: $145 single/$155 double room until April 15 (reservations received after these dates will be honored at the group rate based on availability)
  • If the Adam's Mark Hotel is full, or if you wish to explore other options, you may find the list of hotels/services at http://hotelguide.net/dfw/ and http://dallas.hotelaccommodations.com/ useful. However, do note that there is no formal connection between ACM SIGMOD/PODS and these reservation services nor any other hotels besides the Adam's Mark Dallas Hotel.
Travel Information:
  • Special arrangements were made with American Airlines for discounted air fares ! Call American Airlines at 1-800-221-2255, and say you are with the Association for Computing Machinery (do not mention ACM as American Airlines does not use acronyms !). The reservation agent will ask for a AN number which is, for SIGMOD/PODS 2000, 0850VA.
Local Transportation:
  • Attendees can call Super Shuttle ahead of time to pick them up at the airport, as long as they have their arrival/departure time. The number is 1-800-blue-van (258-3826).
  • Another possibility is to wait until they arrive at the airport. Attendees should then go to the baggage claim area and go over to the phone system, for hotels and shuttles. It will instruct them to dial a 2 digit number and the attendees will need to identify themselves with ACM SIGMOD, going to the Adam's Mark. The dispatcher will then tell them where to go hop on the shuttle.
  • Upon leaving Adam's Mark, the shuttle comes every half hour, beginning on the hour. Or they can call the above number themselves and schedule a specific pick-up time. The concierge at the hotel can schedule a pick up time for you as well.
