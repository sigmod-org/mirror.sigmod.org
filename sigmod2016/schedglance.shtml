SIGMOD/PODS 2016 Program Overview

   Several exciting changes are introduced this year.

  • The number of concurrent tracks is smaller.
    SIGMOD 2016 will only have 3 parallel tracks.
  • Tutorials are pulled out of the main SIGMOD program,
    and they will be held on Sunday, Monday and Friday.
  • Industrial papers will be mixed with SIGMOD research
    papers, and included in the SIGMOD paper sessions.

   Try out Confer, our online interactive conference program.
