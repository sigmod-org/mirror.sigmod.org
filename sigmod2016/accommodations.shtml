Accommodations

Special rates have been negotiated for conference attendees at Hyatt Regency. We provide a special nightly rate of $269 (plus tax) on two types of rooms: King rooms and Double rooms. You can upgrade to other types of rooms based on this price. For example, you can upgrade to bay view rooms by paying $25 more per night.

You can enjoy the special rates for your stay beginning June 23, 2016 through July 4, 2016, based on the hotel availability. To take advantage of the special rates, you should make your reservation by May 28, 2016. The hotel will continue to accept reservations, at the special rate after the cutoff date, pending on the availability of guest rooms. However, we strongly advise conference attendees to make their reservations as early as possible to avoid disappointment and secure these special conference rates.

All room requests require an advance deposit or credit card to guarantee the reservation. Should a guest desire to cancel their reservation, their deposit will be refunded in full if they notify the hotel at least seventy-two (72) hours prior to scheduled arrival date. Credit cards will not be charged until actual check-in or late cancellation.

Make a Reservation

Bookings MUST be made via the following registration link: Hyatt Regency Hotel reservation website.

Please note that the hotel is booked for Tuesday, June 28th, and Wednesday, June 29th.

If you need any assistance of their reservation system, please call 888-421-1442. Please do not use other booking sites, as the special hotel rate for SIGMOD attendees will not be offered there.


To register for the conference, go to the Registration page.
