Conference Venue

The 2020 ACM SIGMOD/PODS Conference will be held in the Double Tree by Hilton Hotel, which is conveniently located within walking distance of the Oregon Convention Center, Moda Center and Portland Memorial Coliseum. Enjoy convenient travel on the Portland MAX light rail, located directly outside this hotel. The hotel offers delightful views of either downtown or the beautiful Cascade Mountain Range.
