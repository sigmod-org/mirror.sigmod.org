      • Commitment to participate in the exhibition
      • Request an exhibition table
      • The number of free registrations you need

    • Please email Berthold Reinwald (reinwald@almaden.ibm.com) and Wen-Syan Li (wen-syan.li@sap.com) if you have any print material that you want to include in the registration package.

  • What are specification of company supplied banners?
    A sponsor can supply banner(s) to be displayed in the conference based on the sponsorship document. We'd like to request the same standard across the board, in order to have a uniform look.

    - Diamond sponsor: 39'' width (84-5/8 height), 2 banners
    - Platinum sponsor: 33'' width (84-5/8'' height), 2 banners
    - Gold sponsor: 22-1/2'' width (59-1/16'' height), 1 banner
    - Silver sponsor: 8-1/2'' width (20-1/16'' height), 1 banner

    - Platinum publisher/exhibitor: 33'' width (84-5/8'' height), 1 banner
    - Gold publisher/exhibitor: 22-1/2'' width (59-1/16'' height), 1 banner
    - Silver publisher: 8-1/2'' width (20-1/16'' height), 1 banner

    One possible vendor for banner is
    http://www.dpsbanners.com/products/13/banner-stands-retractable-trade-show/ whose banner sizes are consistent with the above guideline: http://www.dpsbanners.com/prodimages/BannerBug-BannerStand-Info.pdf .

    A sponsor may also bring a table cloth with logo.
  • What are the sizes for exhibition tables?
    - Diamond sponsors: 2 tables of 6' x 18"
    - Platinum sponsors/exhibitors/publishers: 6' x 30"
    - Gold sponsors/exhibitors/publishers: 8' x 18"
    - Silver sponsors/publishers: 6' x 18"
  • What can be included for a sponsor in the program booklet (A5 size 5.83" x 8.27", color)?
    - Diamond/Platinum sponsor: full page advertisement in program
    - Gold sponsor: half page advertisement in program
    - Silver sponsor: quarter-page advertisement in program

    - Platinum publishers/exhibitors: quarter page advertisement in program
    - Gold publishers/exhibitors: quarter page advertisement in program
  • Are there any wifi during the conference?
    -Yes
  • What the recruiting events (open for Diamond, Platinum, Gold Sponsors) are?
    - We will have two recruiting events!
    • May 21 (Monday) evening, during the SIGMOD reception, qualified sponsors will interact with conference attendees in a social setting about career opportunities.
    • May 22 (Tuesday) 16:45-18:15pm, there will be a "SIGMOD Information Session on Careers in Industry" where qualified sponsors will give a short presentation to attendees. To increase participation, we scheduled this event such that there is no regular SIGMOD research sessions in parallel.
  • What is the exhibition time?
    - The conference is May 21 to May 24, each day starts 8:00am and runs till 6:00pm or so. Sponsors are also welcome to start the exhibition on May 20 (workshop day) starting from 8:00am.
  • Is everyone coming to the conference required a registration?
    - Yes, except one additional "support staff registration" is allowed per sponsor. The "support staff registration" can be used for staff person who stay with the sponsor's exhibition table, but donot attend conference sessions or events.
  • How does a sponsor claim the complimentary registrations and support staff registration?
    - To claim a sponsor's complementary conference registration, please choose "Sponsor" in "Program Selection". To claim a support staff registration, please choose "Sponsor Support Staff" in "Program Selection". Both require a sponsor-specific access code that you have received from our sponsorship chair: Vasilis Vassalos (vasilis@gmail.com). If you have not received it yet, please send your inquires to Vasilis.

We look forward to seeing you soon in Scottsdale!
