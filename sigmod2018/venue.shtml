Conference Venue

Marriott Marquis Houston

Step into the spacious lobby of the downtown Marriott Marquis Houston and know that you've arrived - marble flooring, exquisite chandeliers and gracious service welcome you.
Overlooking Discovery Green Park and connected to the George R Brown Convention Center, the hotel is just minutes from Minute Maid Park.

Contact Information

Marriott Marquis Houston 1777 Walker Street, Houston, TX 77010
+1 713-646-1777

Parking

Off-site parking fee: 8 USD hourly, 27 USD daily
Valet parking: fee: 40 USD daily
Self-parking: nearby in Avenida North Garage / Valet parking for oversized vehicles is USD 48 per night

Event Pavilion

Several social events take place at the Event Pavilion, located in Parkview Terrace next to the Texas shaped lazy river on the 6th floor of Marriott Marquis.
